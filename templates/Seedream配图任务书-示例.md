## 统一视觉规范

### 配色系统(Anthropic 暖色调)

- **米(背景)**:#F0EEE6 — 主背景色,温暖柔和
- **暖墨(线条/文字)**:#2A2825 — 主线条色,深沉但不冷
- **陶橙(强调)**:#DA7756 — 点缀色,引导视线

**色调关键词**:温暖、柔和、人文、专业
**避免**:冷蓝、冷灰、荧光色、高饱和色

### 风格关键词

- **抽象**:不画写实场景,用几何/光影/轮廓表达概念
- **极简**:元素少而精,留白多,不堆砌
- **人文**:有「人」的存在感(轮廓、手势、光影),但不是具体的人脸
- **杂志感**:像 The Economist 或 Monocle 的概念插图

### 共同约束

- ❌ 不出现具体人脸(用轮廓/剪影/手势代替)
- ❌ 不出现文字(文字由排版叠加,Seedream 不要生成)
- ❌ 不出现 logo/品牌标识
- ❌ 不出现科技感的蓝/紫色调
- ✅ 保持暖色调统一
- ✅ 留足负空间(构图不要太满)

### Prompt 描述

```
A minimalist abstract illustration in warm tones (cream #F0EEE6 background, dark charcoal #2A2825 lines, terracotta orange #DA7756 accents).

An abstract clock composed of thin circular arcs and radiating lines, positioned off-center to the right. From the clock's center, streams of small dots and gentle curves flow outward in a spiral pattern, suggesting data flowing through time. The dots are denser in some areas (representing peak usage times) and sparser in others.

The overall mood is calm, professional, and human. No text, no human faces, no realistic objects. Leave plenty of negative space on the left side for text overlay.

Style: editorial illustration, like The Economist or Monocle magazine.
```

### 风格备注

- 时钟不要太写实,用弧线暗示即可
- 数据流要柔和,像烟雾或粒子,不要像电路板
- 左侧留白给标题

| **视觉隐喻** | 人形轮廓的光谱,体现「焦虑不是朝着自己,而是朝着 junior」 |

### Prompt 描述

```
A minimalist abstract illustration in warm tones (cream #F0EEE6 background, dark charcoal #2A2825 lines, terracotta orange #DA7756 accents).

Three abstract human silhouettes of different heights, arranged horizontally from left (shortest) to right (tallest). The shortest silhouette has subtle wave patterns or soft shadows around it, suggesting anxiety or turbulence. The tallest silhouette is surrounded by calm, warm light, suggesting stability and optimism.

The middle silhouette is in transition — some subtle waves, but less than the shortest.

The overall gradient goes from "slightly turbulent" (left) to "calm and warm" (right). No text, no realistic faces or details. Leave negative space for text overlay.

Style: editorial illustration, warm and humanistic, not pessimistic or dystopian.
```

- 整体是温暖的,不是恐惧的
- 和上篇封面形成系列感(都用抽象 + 暖色)

## 图片 3 · 上篇导读 · 节奏图

| 项目 | 内容 |
|---|---|
| **编号** | Fig-1 |
| **标题** | AI 的一天 |
| **放置位置** | 上篇导读末尾,作为「AI 跟着钟表走」的视觉锚点 |
| **尺寸** | 16:9(如 1920×1080 px) |
| **视觉隐喻** | 从凌晨到夜晚的一天流动,光点代表 AI 请求在不同时段的涨落 |

### Prompt 描述

```
A minimalist abstract illustration in warm tones (cream #F0EEE6 background, dark charcoal #2A2825 lines, terracotta orange #DA7756 accents).

A horizontal timeline representing a full day, from dawn (left) to night (right). Along the timeline, abstract shapes and light particles flow and cluster at different points:

- Early morning (left): soft, diffuse particles (sleep requests)
- Mid-morning: slightly denser cluster (work requests)
- Evening (right-center): the densest, brightest cluster (recipe requests at 6pm, 2.3x peak)
- Night (far right): sparse, quiet particles

The particles are warm-toned (terracotta orange and soft gold), flowing like a gentle river of light. The overall mood is calm and observational, like watching a city breathe through a day.

No clock numbers, no text, no realistic objects. Just the flow of time and activity.

Style: editorial illustration, abstract and poetic.
```

### 风格备注

- 不要画具体的时钟或数字
- 用光点的疏密表达「峰值」和「低谷」
- 整体是流动的,像一条河

## 图片 4 · 上篇镜像 · 成本-价值图

| 项目 | 内容 |
|---|---|
| **编号** | Fig-2 |
| **标题** | 成本追踪价值 |
| **放置位置** | 上篇镜像「这告诉你什么」段,作为「AI 使用强度 ≈ 职业价值标记」的视觉锚点 |
| **尺寸** | 16:9(如 1920×1080 px) |
| **视觉隐喻** | 天平或阶梯,一端轻(简单任务),一端重(复杂任务),体现「人在最高价值任务中仍然深度参与」 |

### Prompt 描述

```
A minimalist abstract illustration in warm tones (cream #F0EEE6 background, dark charcoal #2A2825 lines, terracotta orange #DA7756 accents).

An abstract balance scale or stepped structure, with two sides:

- Left side (or lower steps): simple, lightweight geometric shapes (circles, small squares) — representing simple, low-cost tasks
- Right side (or higher steps): complex, layered, dense structures (stacked shapes, intricate patterns) — representing complex, high-value tasks

The heavier side glows with warm terracotta orange, suggesting value and depth. The lighter side is more muted.

A subtle human silhouette or hand is near the heavier side, suggesting "deep participation" — humans are still deeply involved in high-value work.

No text, no realistic objects. Abstract and conceptual.

Style: editorial illustration, clean and professional.
```

### 风格备注

- 天平或阶梯都可以,看 Seedream 哪个效果更自然
- 「重」的一侧要看起来有价值感,不是负担感
- 人的元素要抽象(手势/轮廓),不要写实

- 光谱要柔和过渡,不是硬切
- 人的元素要抽象

## 使用建议

- 生成后先检查色调是否统一(都偏暖,不要有冷色)
- 封面图检查左侧是否有足够留白(给标题叠加)
- 概念图检查是否和正文原报告图「撞车」(不要和原图太像)
- 最终文件命名按上表,放入各篇 `images/` 目录
